# **App Name**: TreinoPro

## Core Features:

- User Authentication: Secure login and registration with Google, email/password, and password recovery.
- Personalized Training Plans: Allow trainers to create and students to access training plans tailored to specific muscle groups and fitness goals.
- Exercise Video Library: A collection of instructional exercise videos for each muscle group. This includes options for trainers to upload custom content to grow the tool.
- Nutritional Guidance: Provide educational content and tips for different dietary goals like weight loss, muscle gain, and pre/post-workout nutrition.  
- Subscription Management: Enable users to subscribe to monthly training plans with recurring payment integration. Offer different tiers (Basic, Premium) with varying features.
- Admin Interface for Trainers: A dashboard for personal trainers to manage training programs, student accounts, payment information, and upload custom videos.
- Progress Tracking Dashboard: Allows students to track workouts and weekly progress to assess the efficacy of their current workout plan.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Saturated purple (#9C27B0) to evoke energy, power, and transformation.
- Background color: Light gray (#F0F0F0) offering a clean and modern canvas for the vibrant details.
- Accent color: Vivid blue (#2962FF) used to highlight CTAs and key interactive elements, creating contrast.
- Headline font: 'Poppins' (sans-serif) for headlines, giving a modern and approachable aesthetic.
- Body font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for body text, for improved readability.
- Large and intuitive icons, easy to understand at a glance, and relevant to fitness, training, and nutrition.
- Clean, intuitive layout with a focus on visual hierarchy to guide users through the app’s features efficiently.
- Subtle transitions and animations to provide feedback on interactions and guide users, maintaining a motivational feel.
